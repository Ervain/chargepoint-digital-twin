Structural and technical design

Preliminary engineering for one bay, which repeats in every configuration. The full-depth roof governs, and the half-depth roof is checked too. Figures are design-stage values to brief a licensed engineer; they are not a stamped design.

Design basis

  • Codes: ADIBC / Dubai Building Code with ASCE 7-16 loads, AISC 360 or EN 1993 steel, ACI 318 concrete, EN 1090-2 EXC2 fabrication.
  • Wind: 45 m/s 3-second gust (ultimate), exposure C, mean height 3.7 m, giving qh = 0.90 kPa. Rechecked at 50 m/s: the members still pass.
  • Roof wind coefficients: monoslope free roof at 7°, clear flow. Envelope CN = ±1.5 on the windward half and ±0.5 on the leeward half, with G = 0.85.
  • Dead load: glass-glass PV 0.13 kPa, plus steel and fascia 0.17 kPa.
  • Roof live load: 0.96 kPa (maintenance), unreduced.
  • Seismic: low hazard, and the small mass means wind governs.
  • Environment: 50 °C ambient, coastal C5 corrosivity.

Low utilisations are expected at this stage: the sizes are set by stiffness, connection room, minimum galvanising thickness and robustness. The engineer can optimise them after fatigue and vortex checks on the fascia.

Base, anchors and foundation

  • Base plate: 1,250 × 950 × 30 mm S355, with stiffeners under each post and grouted 40 mm.
  • Anchors: 8 × M24 grade 8.8, hot-dip galvanised, 500 mm embedment with an anchor frame. 21 kN per bolt against 203 kN capacity (utilisation 0.11).
  • Pad footing: 2.0 × 2.0 × 1.2 m, C40 concrete, T16 bars at 200 mm top and bottom, 75 mm cover, sulphate-resisting cement.
  • Overturning: safety factor 4.5, required ≥ 1.5. The batteries are ignored here because they are removable.
  • Bearing: 53 kPa, against an assumed 150 kPa allowable. The load stays inside the middle third of the footing (e = 0.22 m < B/6).
  • Geotechnical report: required to confirm the allowable bearing, groundwater and sulphates.

Battery column (45 kWh)

  • Footprint: 1,000 × 700 mm, the same as the reference 45 kWh outdoor battery enclosure. The battery bay is 1,500 mm tall, with a 520 mm head compartment above.
  • Battery: 48 V LFP stack with built-in inverter, 5 × 9 kWh modules, about 650 × 440 × 1,350 mm and 420 kg. It is served from the rear door.
  • 45 kWh is below the 50 kWh per-unit threshold in NFPA 855 / IFC 1207. Columns are 1.53 m apart, against the required 0.9 m.
  • Enclosure: double skin, IP55/IP65 gaskets, three-point lock, closed-loop air conditioner, aerosol suppression, gas detection and an explosion vent pointing up and away from the bays.
  • Electrical: DC isolator, external emergency stop and signage per IEC 62619, UL 9540A, and UAE Fire & Life Safety Code Chapter 7/9.

Vehicle safety and clearances

  • Wheel stops: fitted in every bay, 950 mm in front of the battery column (the impact protection IFC 1207.11.3 requires).
  • Headroom: 2.66 m at the low edge of the full roof and 3.1 m on the half-depth roof. Post a 2.5 m height-limit sign.
  • Bays: 2.53 × 5.2 m. One accessible bay per site, 3.7 m wide with an aisle, per the Dubai Universal Design Code.
  • Chargers: stand between the columns with at least 0.6 m clear on each side. Cables are managed so nothing lies on the ground.

Roof, PV and movement

  • PV uplift: 1.14 kPa ultimate, against a module rating of 2.4 kPa uplift and 5.4 kPa down. That is 0.77 kN per clamp across 4 clamps, using the clamp maker's rated clamps.
  • Fascia screens: sit on their own aluminium subframe, IP65, with drainage slots, rated for the same wind pressure.
  • Thermal movement: 5.5 mm over a 4-bay roof, so there is a slotted expansion joint in the fascia at every bay.
  • Rainwater: the 7° slope drains to the low edge into a concealed gutter, with a downpipe inside one strut sleeve.

Materials and durability

  • Steel: S355J2, hot-dip galvanised to ISO 1461 (85 µm minimum), then polyester powder coat to ISO 12944 C5-H (duplex system).
  • Fixings: A4 stainless steel, with isolation washers between dissimilar metals.
  • Earthing and lightning: each column has its own earth pit (bonded at 10 Ω or less), and the structure is bonded for lightning per IEC 62305.
  • Design life: 25 years for the structure, 10–15 years for the batteries (replaceable through the rear door).

Before construction

  • Stamped calculations and drawings by a licensed UAE structural engineer, plus the municipality building permit.
  • A geotechnical investigation at the site.
  • Civil Defence approval of the battery system, with UL 9540A test data from the battery supplier.
  • ADDC/DEWA approval for the chargers and grid connection; IEC 61851 and IEC 60364-7-722 compliance.
  • Shop drawings and a connection design by the fabricator (EN 1090 EXC2), and an inspection and test plan.
